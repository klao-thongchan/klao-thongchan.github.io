The operating problem

Multi-step AI can save time, but opaque outputs and hidden failure modes make it difficult for teams to trust or adopt.

My role

I am independently exploring agent systems that plan, act, verify, and report, together with retrieval pipelines and lightweight evaluation harnesses.

The solution pattern

  • Retrieve task-specific context before the workflow acts.
  • Expose intermediate steps and evidence for inspection.
  • Evaluate accuracy, tone, localization quality, and failure modes.
  • Keep a human review point before consequential output is used.

Current status

This is active research focused on workflow architecture, evaluation, and adoption—not a production deployment or quantified business outcome.

FDE / SE signal: framing AI around an operator’s workflow, observable failure modes, and a credible path to deployment.
